# Lineage

AgenticGoat was seeded from [aminrj-labs/mcp-attack-labs](https://github.com/aminrj-labs/mcp-attack-labs) at commit `a5ef3f931bd5a87c41c2eddafc70fb606f2d2f9c` on 24 September 2026.

The two projects diverge from here. `mcp-attack-labs` remains the upstream raw lab bench; AgenticGoat is the packaged, framework-mapped, browser-runnable distribution. Cross-pollination between them is manual.

The full git history of the bench is carried in this repository, so authorship and timeline are preserved. Nothing in `mcp-attack-labs` was renamed, redirected, or modified as part of the seeding.
